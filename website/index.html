03 / INTERPRETATION

Counts are not risk rates

Higher counts alone do not prove a road is more dangerous. Population, vehicle numbers, road length and travel exposure are not controlled in this dataset.

Source: PRS India, “Demand for Grants 2026–27: Road Transport and Highways”, Table 15, citing Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH), Road Accidents in India. Data collection page: data.opencity.in/dataset/road-accidents-in-india-2024.
Method note: State-wise accident counts for 2020–2024. This dataset does not contain accident hour/month, cause, injuries or fatalities by state; those analyses require compatible additional data. Missing values are not treated as zero. R forecasts are exploratory only because there are only five annual observations.
